Hussain Hadah

Hussain Hadah

Labor, health, and education economist

I am a lead research economist at Northwestern University’s Research and Innovation for Social and Economic Inclusion Lab. I use causal inference methods, field experiments, and administrative data to study discrimination, ethnic identity, immigration, mental health, education, and labor markets.

I am also a research affiliate at the IZA Institute of Labor Economics and Tulane University’s Murphy Institute. Before joining Northwestern, I was a postdoctoral fellow at Tulane from 2023 to 2026. I earned my PhD in Economics from the University of Houston in 2023.

    Hussain Hadah

  • Immigration Enforcement and Hispanic Identity

    Hussain Hadah and Augustine Denteh

    Under review.

    Secure Communities reduces self-reported Hispanic identity on average, with declines among first- and third-generation children but increased identification among second-generation children.

    • Preprint

  • The Effects of Waiting Periods on Firearm Suicides in the U.S.

    Hussain Hadah, Gael Compta, and Ali Saffouri

    Under review.

    Firearm-purchase waiting periods reduce firearm suicides by 0.92 deaths per 100,000, or 12 percent, overall, with larger reductions for men, White adults, and adults aged 55 and older.

    • Preprint
    • IZA DP No. 18624

  • Immigration Enforcement, Sanctuary Cities, and Rising Hispanic Suicide Rates

    Hussain Hadah

    Under review.

    Secure Communities increases suicide rates among Hispanic adults aged 34 and older by 0.11 per 100,000 overall, with increases among men and decreases among women.

    • Preprint
    • IZA DP No. 18613
